**from $275/page**, under a Copy & Content group in Individual Services.

must reach, which is not what it means.

than the paid service: tightening supplied wording, restructuring, editing, and
turning supplied business information into usable website copy are part of every
website engagement, and small edits after launch are part of Site Care. The paid
service applies only where Kreated substantially researches and writes original
content from limited material.
