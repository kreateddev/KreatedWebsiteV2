# PROOF

**Everything Kreated may claim, and everything it may not.**

This is the trust engine. It outranks aesthetics, copy elegance and conversion tactics. Read it
before writing a single word of V2 copy.

Imported from `KreatedWebsiteV1` (`docs/SEO_AND_CONTENT.md` §4, `START_HERE.md` §6,
`docs/PROJECT_OVERVIEW.md` §5), read-only inspection 2026-08-19.

## 1. Evidence classes

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Independently observed** | Kreated checked it directly and recorded date, query and location |
| **Client reported** | The client said it. Not verified by Kreated. Must stay visibly labelled |
| **Testimonial** | A real published review, quoted exactly |
| **Project fact** | Scope, dates, deliverables — verifiable from the engagement itself |
| **Unverified / pending** | Not usable as proof |

⚠️ **Correction, Skyler 2026-08-19 (DECISION 017).** An earlier version of this document overstated

**🚫 FORBIDDEN**
- Review-**count** framing — `1 review`, `1 review on Google`, any count display

## 6. Site-wide prohibitions — absolute

🚫 **No invented anything.** No metrics, percentages, scores, counts, traffic figures, revenue,
ratings, review counts, awards, certifications, logo walls, "trusted by", clients, outcomes or
locations.

🚫 **No `aggregateRating`, `Review`, `Award`, `ratingValue`, `reviewCount` or testimonial schema**
until they are real and Skyler approves. V1 verified **zero occurrences site-wide** and that should
remain true in V2.

⚠️ **DECISION 017 does not unlock rating schema.** Permitting `Rated 5 Stars` or star glyphs as an
evidence-backed *visual* treatment is a separate question from emitting rating **structured data**,
which triggers rich-result eligibility and a much higher evidentiary bar. Schema stays forbidden.

🚫 **Nothing may imply a team.** No staff, no departments, no "we're a team of…".

🚫 **Kreated cannot bypass, guarantee or control Google's verification decision.** *Guidance* and
*help with* are the only honest verbs.

🚫 **A recreation is fabrication.** Proof must be a real screenshot of a real live site, or nothing.
V1 deleted hand-drawn SVG "screenshots" for exactly this reason. Mockups, browser illustrations,
gradient fills and AI-generated imagery must never sit in a proof position.

🚫 **Honest unfinished states.** Work in progress is labelled in progress. A missing asset is a
marked, designed slot — never a fake.

**RULED, Skyler 2026-08-19 (DECISION 009).**

V1's homepage carried a marquee kicker: **"Brands We've Scaled"** across LearnSmart, LLEC and Rare
Raleigh. V1's own inline record flagged it as an open honesty exception (defect #22): *"scaled" is a
results claim, and no growth metric exists for any of the three marks.* Phase 1 removed it; Skyler
restored it on 2026-08-03; **Skyler has now dropped it for V2.**

🚫 **The phrase "Brands We've Scaled" does not carry forward and is not approved copy for V2.**

**Reason as stated:** "scaled" creates a growth/results implication stronger than the documented
evidence.

⚠️ This resolves V1 defect #22 for V2 purposes. It says nothing about *whether* client marks appear
on the V2 site — **client/project presentation is reconsidered after creative-concept approval.**
Any replacement kicker or client-rail treatment is `COPY CANDIDATE — NOT APPROVED` until then, and
must not smuggle the same results implication back in under different words ("Brands We've Grown",
"Businesses We've Transformed", etc. are the same claim).
