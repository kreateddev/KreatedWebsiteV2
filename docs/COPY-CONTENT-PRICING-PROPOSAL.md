## 1. The boundary, precisely

The reason this is worth pricing at all is that Kreated already promises
something called "copy support" in every website engagement, and the gap between
that promise and *writing the site* is where scope quietly disappears.

### Copy assistance — already included, already paid for

Named on `/pricing/` in every website package as **Copy support**:

- editing what you already have
- tightening it
- deciding the order of a page and what goes above the fold
- turning documents, emails and a conversation into usable page copy
- writing the headlines and the calls to action

The defining property: **the client supplied the substance.** Kreated is
shaping material that exists.

### Full Copy & Content Writing — a separate service

- researching the subject well enough to write about it credibly
- writing original page content from a brief and an interview rather than from
  a document
- developing service and location content that has to be specific enough to
  justify its own page
- building a way of describing the business that did not exist before

The defining property: **there was nothing to shape.** Kreated is producing the
substance, which is a different job with a different cost and a different risk.

⚠ The honest test is not word count. It is: *if the client vanished for three
weeks, could this page still be written?* If yes, it is writing. If no, it is
assistance.

## 3. Recommended model

**Per page, with a project minimum, quoted from a page inventory.**

Not per word: it rewards padding, and thin-but-right is the goal on most of
these pages. Not hourly: it makes the client pay for research they cannot see
and turns every revision into a negotiation. Per page is also the only model
that composes with the rest of `/pricing/`, where everything else is priced by
the thing being bought.

### Proposed figures

| Item | Proposed | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard page written from scratch** (about, contact, a simple service page) | **from $275** | Sits just above the $250 Additional Standard Page, which is the build without the words. The pair reads correctly: build $250, build and write $525. |
| **Researched service page** | **from $400** | Interview, research, and copy specific enough to be found for that service. |
| **Location page copy** | **from $450** | The content is the entire justification for a location page existing. Under-pricing this produces the thin pages `/pricing/` says Kreated will not build. |
| **Landing page copy** | **from $550** | Conversion structure, not description. Below the $750 specialist floor, above the freelance one. |
| **Homepage** | **from $650** | The hardest page and the one that sets the voice for everything else. |
| **Project minimum** | **$1,200** | Below this the research overhead is not recoverable. It also keeps this from becoming a stream of $275 one-page jobs, which is the failure mode of per-page pricing. |
| **Full site content, 8–10 pages** | **$2,400–$3,800** | Lands inside the observed $1,600–$4,000 mid-level band and below the $5,000 agency floor, which is the correct position. |

### One alternative worth considering

**Bundle it as a package modifier instead**, priced as a percentage uplift on
the website engagement: *Launch + full content, Growth + full content.* It is
simpler to quote and impossible to mis-scope, but it hides what writing costs,
and it cannot be bought by someone who already has a site. My recommendation is
per page.
