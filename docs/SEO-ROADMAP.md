**Not "more pages".** Three structural problems, two of which need no new page at all:

## 4. Competitive patterns worth acting on

1. **Two SERP tiers.** Unmodified vertical and cost queries belong to national specialists and
   high-authority publishers. Locally-modified and question-shaped queries belong to small
   agencies. Kreated competes in the second tier, not the first.
2. **Depth is not the moat.** The pages winning question queries are ordinary agency posts.
   Kreated's existing service pages are already 1,360–2,116 words, comparable or longer.
3. **Everyone hedges on price.** Kreated's published price table is a structural advantage that
   costs nothing to use.
4. **Proof is thin across the field.** Most competitors show logos and adjectives. Kreated has
   three named engagements with dated, labeled scope.
5. **A blog post outranked agencies on a commercial query** (§3c). Informational content is a
   legitimate commercial route here, not a consolation prize.
6. **AEO claims are aggressive and unsupported.** Kreated's refusal to promise citations is a
   differentiator in a field making promises, provided the page is actually found.

## 6. Location pages — the ruling stands

`docs/SEO.md` already forbids city pages without a real delivery basis. Nothing in this
research changes that, and the SERP evidence argues against it: the Raleigh pages that rank are
substantive service pages, not thin city variants.

**A city page may be proposed only when all four hold:** distinct demand exists for that city;
Kreated genuinely targets it; a page can be written that is not Raleigh copy with a name
swapped; and there is at least one real local engagement to reference.

Today, zero cities meet all four. 🚫 No city pages in this batch, and no city-page farm ever.

## 8. Resource architecture

### `/resources/` over `/blog/` — recommended

| | `/resources/` | `/blog/` |
|---|---|---|
| Matches positioning | Reference material a buyer consults while deciding | Implies personality, opinion, cadence |
| Publishing pressure | None. Four durable pages are a complete resource section | A blog with four posts and no new ones reads abandoned |
| Dates | Not expected, so a page does not visibly age | Dated posts decay in perceived freshness |
| Fits the site | The site has no author system, no post dates, no categories | Would need all three |

**Recommendation: `/resources/<slug>/`,** with a `/resources/` index once three or more pages
exist. Below three, the index is thinner than the pages it lists; link them from the service
pages instead.

**Navigation:** 🚫 do not add Resources to the desktop header in this batch. The header is four
items and its restraint is deliberate. Add `/resources/` to the footer **Pages** column, which
already carries `/method/` on the same basis.

## 13. Content quality standard for every future resource

1. **Answer in the first paragraph.** No throat-clearing about the importance of the internet.
2. **Original analysis**, not a synthesis of the nine posts already ranking.
3. **Written to an owner-operator**, not to a marketing manager.
4. **Concrete examples** from local service work.
5. **Distinguish situations** — "it depends" is only acceptable when the page then says on what.
6. **No keyword stuffing**, no padded intro, no "in today's digital landscape".
7. **Connect to business impact** in the owner's terms: jobs, calls, quotes.
8. **A reason to exist beyond the keyword.** If the only argument is search volume, do not write it.

### AEO compatibility, without gimmicks

Clear definitions, a direct answer near the top, factual structure, headings that state
propositions rather than tease, unambiguous entity naming (Kreated, Raleigh, the named
services). These are the same properties that make the page good for a human.

🚫 No "AI SEO" devices. 🚫 No schema added merely because a page is meant to be AEO-friendly —
`docs/SEO.md` restricts schema to what visible content supports, and that rule holds.
