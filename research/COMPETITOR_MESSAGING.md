🚫 **No visual design was studied.** Per Skyler's instruction this covers positioning, offers, proof,
CTAs, trust strategy and language only. Nothing about layout, colour, type or motion was recorded.

⚠️ **Sample bias, stated up front:** these four were found by searching the term Kreated wants to
own. That selects for agencies **good at SEO** — which is a biased sample for judging what a typical
competitor sounds like, and an unbiased sample for judging **who Kreated will actually be compared
against in search results.** The second is what matters here.

## What nobody says

The white space. Each is a gap in the sample of four — **not proof of a market-wide gap**.

1. **Nobody shows the diagnosis before the pitch.** Everyone offers a free audit; nobody demonstrates
   diagnostic ability *on the page itself*. The audit is a form, not a display of thinking.
2. **Nobody states what they will not claim.** Every proof point is asserted flat, with no evidence
   grade. In a market where **>60% of clients end up unhappy** (C10), unqualified certainty is the
   sound of the thing that burned them.
3. **Nobody names the invisible loss.** No one explains that deals are lost during the customer's
   private research (C1) — the buyer's actual, unseen problem.
4. **Nobody addresses the freeze.** Two publish prices, which helps; none acknowledges the $500-to-
   $30,000 vertigo directly.
5. **Nobody says who they are wrong for.** All four are for everyone.
6. **Nobody shows unfinished work honestly.** Portfolios are uniformly complete and successful —
   ⚠️ which is precisely the shape Kreated's evidence *cannot* take, and might therefore be an
   advantage rather than the liability it looks like.

## The strategic reading

The Raleigh market is **loud, claim-heavy and internally identical**. Everyone asserts results,
everyone offers a free audit, everyone promises access and no lock-in, half publish prices, and the
proof currency is volume.

Kreated cannot win any of those contests. Three clients, one review, no metrics, no tenure.

🔴 **But the research says those contests are already discounted** — >60% of agency clients are
unhappy within a year (C10), 38% switch annually (C10), and buyers arrive with "hard-won
scepticism" (C10). **The market is saturated with unverifiable confidence.**

The two things Kreated has that nobody in the sample has:
1. **Bounded, dated, verifiable evidence** — the LLEC observation is a specific claim with a date, a
   query and a location, and stated limits. Nobody else limits a claim.
2. **A visibly better-made website**, which is the one criterion judged in 50ms before any claim is
   read (A3) — and the only criterion requiring no clients, no tenure and no metrics.

⚠️ Both are **execution**, not messaging. Neither is a creative direction. Step 3 owns that.
