# TRUST REQUIREMENTS

What the homepage must prove before a visitor will contact Kreated, ranked by evidence.

Evidence grades per [`SOURCES.md`](SOURCES.md).

---

## The ordering principle

Trust requirements are not a checklist — they are **sequential**. A visitor who has not cleared
requirement 1 never evaluates requirement 4. The order below is derived from the evidence, not from
convention.

🔴 **Requirement 0 happens before any content is read, and it is the only one that cannot be
argued.**

---

## 0. "This was made by someone competent" — proved by execution, not claims
**Evidence: HIGH (A3, A4, A5). Timing: ~50ms, pre-verbal.**

Credibility is judged in about **50 milliseconds**, and that judgment correlates strongly with
judgments made at 500ms (A3). **94% of first-impression feedback concerns design** (A4); identical
content is judged more credible when presented with higher aesthetic quality (A5).

For a web design agency this is not a soft factor. **The site is the deliverable, on display.** A
buyer who cannot evaluate schema, page speed or search strategy *can* evaluate whether the artefact
in front of them was made by someone who knows what they are doing — and does so before reading.

**This is the only trust requirement that:**
- requires no clients, no metrics, no tenure, no reviews;
- Kreated can win outright against every competitor in the sample;
- cannot be faked with copy.

⚠️ It is also **the single point of failure.** If the site is not visibly excellent, every honest,
carefully bounded claim below is read by someone who has already decided.

---

## 1. "This is for a business like mine"
**Evidence: HIGH (A6, E1–E4). Timing: seconds.**

Relevance is the first *read* judgment. All four competitors put local + service-business framing at
the top (E1–E4) — it is the filter everyone passes, so failing it is fatal and passing it earns
nothing.

⚠️ **Complication for Kreated:** evidence is regional, not city-local. LLEC is Wilmington;
LearnSmart is Raleigh/Durham/Chapel Hill. V1's line already hedges — *"Based in Raleigh. Built for
businesses anywhere."* — which is honest but weaker as a relevance signal than a competitor's
unqualified "Raleigh".

---

## 2. "They understand what's actually wrong — better than I can say it"
**Evidence: HIGH (C1, B8, C10). Timing: first scroll.**

🔴 **The strongest opportunity identified in Step 2.**

The buyer cannot diagnose their own problem. **73% of SMB owners are not confident their marketing
is working** (B8) — they have the symptom without the cause. Deals are lost during the customer's
private research, which the owner never observes (C1).

And **nobody in the competitor sample does this** ([`COMPETITOR_MESSAGING.md`](COMPETITOR_MESSAGING.md)
"What nobody says" #1 and #3). Everyone offers a free audit; nobody demonstrates diagnostic ability
on the page itself. The audit is a form to fill in, not evidence of thinking.

Why this beats claiming results: a buyer with "hard-won scepticism" (C10) discounts every assertion
about outcomes, but **cannot discount an accurate description of their own situation.** Being
correctly diagnosed is self-verifying — the buyer checks it against their own experience instantly,
at no risk.

⚠️ Kreated has done exactly this for a real client: **Rare Raleigh was an audit-and-plan engagement
before any build** ([`../docs/PROJECTS.md`](../docs/PROJECTS.md) §3). The diagnosis is the asset —
and it is one of the two approved homepage Work candidates.

---

## 3. "They can actually execute — this is real, live work"
**Evidence: MEDIUM-HIGH (A6). Timing: mid-page.**

"Case studies, client tenure, and consistent reporting do more to build confidence than flashy
pitches" (A6) — note **case studies**, i.e. an engagement explained, not a screenshot grid.

Kreated's assets: two live, real, screenshottable sites (`learnsmart.dev`,
`leaklocatorseastcoast.com`), captured genuinely, with recorded provenance and an explicit no-mockup
policy ([`../docs/ASSETS.md`](../docs/ASSETS.md) §4).

🔴 **Direct tension with [`../docs/DECISIONS.md`](../docs/DECISIONS.md) 007 and 008.** Of the two
approved homepage Work candidates:
- **LLEC** — real, live, screenshottable, but has **no case-study narrative** by design;
- **Rare Raleigh** — has the narrative, but **no completed implementation** and may never imply one.

The project that satisfies this requirement most completely — LearnSmart, the only full case study,
the only live site with a testimonial and a reported outcome — is deliberately **not** a homepage
default. **This requirement is therefore the one the current homepage plan serves least well.**
Recorded for Skyler in [`STEP_2_SYNTHESIS.md`](STEP_2_SYNTHESIS.md) §15, not overridden here.

## 4. "A specific person is accountable, and reachable"
**Evidence: HIGH as a criterion (A6), but 🔴 NOT differentiating (E2, E3).**

"Honesty, professionalism, and reliability from the people a business will actually work with
continue to outweigh flash or even raw experience" (A6).

⚠️ **Already claimed by competitors, more plainly:**
- E2: *"You won't be passed to account managers — you'll work with me on strategy and results."*
- E3: *"You get my cell phone number."*

**Necessary. Not sufficient. Not a positioning platform.** Kreated must clear this bar; it cannot
win on it.

---

## 5. "They'll actually finish"
**Evidence: MEDIUM (C8, C12). Timing: late, pre-contact.**

Big-agency failure mode: "big promises in the sales call... and radio silence after launch" (C8).
Freelancer failure mode: no backup, no supervision, no continuity (C12).

🔴 **Kreated carries the freelancer version of this fear at agency-adjacent prices** — structurally
its weakest trust position ([`OBJECTIONS.md`](OBJECTIONS.md) #7, #9).

Two under-used counter-assets, both already in Kreated's own materials:
1. The LearnSmart engagement finished **start to finish in two weeks**, volunteered by the client
   unprompted ([`../docs/PROOF.md`](../docs/PROOF.md) §5).
2. Rare Raleigh's incomplete state is **openly labelled** rather than concealed — evidence of a
   habit of telling the truth about status, which is exactly the behaviour a burned buyer is
   scanning for.

⚠️ **Asset 2 only works if the buyer reads it as honesty rather than as failure.** Which reading
they take is decided by requirement 0 — a visibly excellent site makes "unfinished, and labelled"
read as rigour; a mediocre one makes it read as an excuse.

---

## 6. "I won't be trapped, and I'll own what I pay for"
**Evidence: MEDIUM (E1, E3, C10). Timing: late.**

Universal competitor pre-emption — "No contracts. Cancel anytime." (E1), "No Lock-In Contracts",
"You own your website and all your SEO work", "We don't hold anything hostage" (E3) — against a
documented industry reality of "vague retainers, hidden fees, rigid contracts" (C10).

**Table stakes.** Its absence is noticed; its presence is not credited.

---

## 7. "This will produce business value"
**Evidence: 🔴 LOW as a *provable* requirement, HIGH as a *desire*.**

The buyer wants this proved and **it cannot be**, by anyone, honestly. Everyone claims it — "$10M+
Revenue Generated", "372% increase in leads" (E1) — and **>60% of the clients who believed those
claims were unhappy within a year** (C10).

🔴 **The most important strategic finding in this file:** attempting to satisfy requirement 7 with
claims moves Kreated into the exact category the buyer distrusts, and Kreated would compete there
from the weakest possible position — three clients, one review, no metrics.

**The honest substitute is requirement 2.** A buyer who believes you correctly diagnosed the problem
extends provisional belief that you can fix it. **Diagnosis is the credible route to a claim about
value; assertion is not.**

⚠️ Kreated's one usable outcome — the LLEC observation — is bounded to a query, a location and a
date, with an undocumented result surface ([`../docs/PROOF.md`](../docs/PROOF.md) §2). It is real and
it is small. Its persuasive power comes from **being limited when nobody else limits anything**.

## Requirements the brief listed that the research does not support well

| Listed | Verdict |
|---|---|
| "Kreated is real" | ⚠️ **Not a live concern.** No evidence buyers doubt existence. Real name, real phone, real photo handle it incidentally. Do not spend page on it |
| "There is a clear process" | Supporting, not load-bearing. Process appears in criteria sets (A6) but always behind people, transparency and evidence. ⚠️ Note V1 led with the Kreated Method; research puts it at #5 support, not #2 headline |
| "Kreated understands service businesses" | Real, but it is a **component of requirement 2**, not separate. Understanding the *category* is worth little; naming *their* specific problem is worth a lot |

## The trust sequence

```
0  Competence          ← proved by the artefact, not stated. 50ms. Pre-verbal
1  Relevance           ← "for a business like mine"
2  Diagnosis           ← 🔴 THE OPPORTUNITY. Nobody else does this
3  Capability          ← real live work. ⚠️ weakened by DECISION 007
4  Accountability      ← necessary, not differentiating
5  Completion          ← Kreated's structural weak point
6  No entrapment       ← table stakes
7  Business value      ← cannot be proved; earned via 2, never asserted
```

**Zero and two are where Kreated wins. Three and five are where it is exposed. Four, six and seven
are where it can only draw.**
