# OFFER FIT

How Kreated's five services map to actual buyer problems.

🚫 **No service is renamed, merged, split, added or dropped** — forbidden by
[`../docs/SERVICES.md`](../docs/SERVICES.md) §7. This file assesses fit only.

Evidence grades per [`SOURCES.md`](SOURCES.md).

## 1. Comprehension — how easily the buyer understands each service

| Service | Buyer comprehension | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Web Design & Development** | 🟢 **Immediate** | Concrete noun. Produces a visible artefact. No translation needed |
| **Website Redesign** | 🟢 **Immediate** | Even clearer — the buyer already knows their site is old |
| **Google Business Profile Optimization** | 🟡 **Moderate** | They know the thing (their Google listing); "optimization" is the vague part. ⚠️ **Uniquely self-verifiable** — GBP has an owner-facing dashboard, so the buyer can check their own numbers without trusting anyone |
| **Local SEO** | 🟠 **Poor, and contaminated** | Understood as a category, distrusted as a purchase. Carries the full weight of the trust deficit — "black box" reports, 38% annual provider switching (C10) |
| **Brand Strategy & Identity** | 🔴 **Worst** | Abstract, no visible deliverable, no obvious business outcome. ⚠️ **But note:** Kreated's one real testimonial names *"my new website and branding/logo"* as a single purchase — the buyer understood it fine **as an attachment to a website**, never as a standalone |

🔴 **The pattern:** comprehension tracks **visibility of deliverable**, not value. Brand Strategy may
be the highest-leverage service and is the hardest to sell alone.

## 3. What is commonly bought together

**Evidence: MEDIUM. Basis is Kreated's own three engagements plus competitor bundling (E1–E4).**

| Bundle | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Website + Brand identity** | 🟢 Strongest. LearnSmart bought Brand identity · Website · Local SEO · Analytics. The client herself describes it as one thing: *"my new website and branding/logo"* |
| **Website + Google Business Profile** | 🟢 Strong. LLEC's exact approved scope is `Website · Google Business Profile` ([`../docs/PROOF.md`](../docs/PROOF.md) §2). Also logical: **56% of GBP actions are website clicks** (B2) — a good profile sends traffic to a bad site |
| **Redesign + Local SEO** | 🟡 Rare Raleigh's scoped plan, not yet delivered |
| **Local SEO + GBP** | 🟡 Sold together by every competitor (E1–E3). ⚠️ Also the pair most at risk of reading as one thing sold twice |

🔴 **Both strongest bundles are anchored by a website.** In Kreated's real history, **the website is
the entry purchase and everything else attaches to it.** Brand Strategy and Local SEO have never
been sold alone.

## 5. What leads naturally to the Free Website Audit

**Evidence: MEDIUM-HIGH.**

The audit's natural entry point is **Website Redesign** — an existing site the buyer already
suspects is failing. Website Redesign is:
- the most comprehensible service (§1),
- attached to the best-evidenced trigger territory ([`PAINS_AND_TRIGGERS.md`](PAINS_AND_TRIGGERS.md)),
- the one where the buyer already suspects a problem but cannot name it.

**Web Design (no site yet)** does not fit — there is nothing to audit.

🔴 **But the strongest version is broader than one service.** The buyer's real problem is *losing
business invisibly* ([`PAINS_AND_TRIGGERS.md`](PAINS_AND_TRIGGERS.md) §1), which spans site, profile
and search. **An audit scoped to "your website" under-serves what the buyer needs told**, while an
audit scoped to everything risks becoming the five-service upsell in disguise.

⚠️ **Named tension, unresolved by design.** The public name is fixed as `Free Website Audit`
([`../docs/DECISIONS.md`](../docs/DECISIONS.md) 010). Scope is a Skyler decision, flagged in
[`AUDIT_RESEARCH.md`](AUDIT_RESEARCH.md) and [`STEP_2_SYNTHESIS.md`](STEP_2_SYNTHESIS.md) §16.

## 6. Fit against the two primary buyer profiles

| Service | Profile A — Established Trade Operator | Profile B — Professional Practice |
|---|---|---|
| Web Design & Development | 🟡 Has a site already | 🟢 **Strong** — often no site at all |
| Website Redesign | 🟢 **Strongest fit** | 🟡 Situational |
| Local SEO | 🟢 Strong — map pack decides who gets called | 🟠 Weaker — referral/reputation-driven |
| Google Business Profile | 🟢 **Strong** — verifiable, low-commitment entry | 🟡 Moderate |
| Brand Strategy & Identity | 🟠 Hardest sell — sounds like a luxury to a trade operator | 🟢 **Strong** — credibility *is* the product |

🔴 **The five services split cleanly along the two profiles, and the split is diagonal.** Redesign +
Local SEO + GBP serve Profile A; Web Design + Brand Strategy serve Profile B. **Almost nothing
serves both strongly.**

⚠️ Sharpened by [`../docs/DECISIONS.md`](../docs/DECISIONS.md) 007: the homepage Work candidates
(LLEC, Rare Raleigh) are **both Profile A**, while the strongest Profile B proof (LearnSmart) is off
the homepage. **The homepage will therefore skew hard to Profile A** — coherent, but it means Web
Design and Brand Strategy will be the least-supported services on the page.

## 7. 🔴 Offer gaps found — for Skyler, not for Step 2 to fix

### Gap 1 — AI search visibility is unnamed
**Evidence: HIGH (A1, A2, E1, E3).**

Consumers using AI for local recommendations went **6% → 45% in one year**; **42% trust AI
recommendations as much as written reviews**; **Google's share of local discovery fell 83% → 71%**
(A1, A2).

Two of four competitors already sell against this — *"dominate Google & AI search"* (E1), *"show up
when locals search Google or AI?"* (E3). **None of Kreated's five services names it.**

⚠️ Arguably inside Local SEO's existing scope, which would be a **scope clarification, not a new
service**. That distinction is Skyler's to make.

### Gap 2 — no paid-media offering
**Evidence: MEDIUM (E1, C5).**

E1 sells Google Ads and Local Service Ads. A balanced contractor lead mix is roughly **40% paid, 30%
organic/GBP, 20% referral, 10% other** (C5) — so Kreated addresses ~30% of where this buyer's leads
actually come from.

**Not a recommendation to add a service.** A relevant limit on what Kreated can claim about lead
generation overall.

### Gap 3 — nothing addresses speed-to-lead
**Evidence: HIGH (C6, C1).**

**95% of home-services companies fail to respond to a lead within five minutes; 40% never respond at
all; 78% of customers buy from the first responder** (C6, C1).

🔴 **This is the most consequential finding for Kreated's delivery risk.** A perfect site delivering
leads to a business that does not answer produces no result — and the client will attribute the
failure to Kreated. It is a **qualification criterion** ([`BEST_FIT_CLIENT.md`](BEST_FIT_CLIENT.md))
and an honesty opportunity: saying plainly that a website cannot fix an unanswered phone is the kind
of statement no competitor in the sample makes.
