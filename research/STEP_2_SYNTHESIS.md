## 1. Primary buyer

**The Established Trade Operator.** Owner-operated local service business — restoration, roofing,
HVAC, plumbing, electrical, pool/leak, remodeling — where **one job is worth $3,000+** and revenue
runs roughly **$500k–$5M**.

The owner decides personally; no marketing person exists. **56% of SMBs have ≤1hr/day for

## 3. The most expensive problem in their mind

**Business is being lost during the customer's private research, and the owner never sees it
happen.**

**97% of consumers read reviews; 41% always do** (A1). Homeowners start with AI, validate through
reviews, check the website, then contact a shortlist — **most deals are lost during private
research, not at the sales table** (C1). **98% search online to find local businesses**, up from 90%
in 2019 (C7).

🔴 The owner experiences this only as *the phone being quieter*, with no cause attached. **They have
the symptom and cannot reach the diagnosis.**

→ [`PAINS_AND_TRIGGERS.md`](PAINS_AND_TRIGGERS.md) §1

## 4. Strongest emotional tension

**REVISED 2026-08-19 — there are two, and they belong to two different populations.**

**Triggered buyer** (arrives by referral or search): *"We look smaller and less capable than we
actually are — and I can't tell how much it's costing me."* Credibility is judged in **~50
milliseconds** (A3); **94% of first-impression feedback concerns design** (A4); identical content is
judged more credible with better presentation (A5). ⚠️ Mechanism peer-reviewed; that embarrassment
*initiates* purchases is not evidenced.

🔴 **Untriggered buyer** (who Kreated actually cold-calls): *"Things are working. I don't want to open
this file."* — evidenced first-party by *"we're consistent / content with where we're at."* Not
ignorance; a **defended position**, frequently correct, and **actively hostile to being told there is
a problem**.

⚠️ **The two require opposite handling.** The first wants the loss named. The second experiences the
same statement as an accusation. **Only checkable facts serve both** — their own GBP numbers, their
own review recency, what a customer actually sees.

## 13. Free Website Audit — role

**Format works:** audits convert "often above 40% on targeted traffic"; specificity beats vagueness —
*"Get My Free 30-Minute Audit" outperforms "Book a Call"* (C11).

🔴 **Offering one is not a differentiator — 3 of 4 competitors already do** (E1, E2, E4).

🔴 **The phrase is contaminated.** "Free website audit" is the standard opener of the cold-outreach
industry this buyer already deflects.

**What would make it work:** it tells them something true they didn't know, verifiably (their own GBP
numbers against the **59 actions/month** benchmark), quickly, **with stated limits** — the one habit
no competitor has.

**What must never be promised:** rankings, guarantees, invented metrics or scores, an implied team,
AI capability, results-you-can-expect.

→ [`AUDIT_RESEARCH.md`](AUDIT_RESEARCH.md)

### 13a. 🔴 The audit's second job, and a directional problem

**Strengthened:** *"Send me some information"* (F1 item 5) is a **request for exactly this artefact**.
The audit is not only an inbound conversion device — it is the answer to Kreated's most common
cold-call outcome.

**Complicated:** *"send me information"* means **you send, I receive.** The audit asks the prospect to
**submit first**. Opposite directions. ⚠️ **A sendable artefact and a requestable one may not be the
same object.**

**Also complicated:** the word *audit* presupposes fault, and lands on a buyer who has just said they
are content and already have a website. ⚠️ The name is fixed and this is not a request to change it —
but the audit must contain **checkable facts, not verdicts**, and **"nothing much is wrong" must be a
possible outcome.** An audit that can only find problems is a sales instrument, and this buyer knows
the genre.

→ [`AUDIT_RESEARCH.md`](AUDIT_RESEARCH.md) §8a

## 14. Recommended persuasion order

```
0  COMPETENCE     proved by the artefact, pre-verbal, ~50ms
1  RELEVANCE      "for a business like mine"
2  DIAGNOSIS      "they see what I can't"              ← 🔴 THE HINGE
3  COST           "this is costing me real money"      ← the buyer's own arithmetic
4  CAPABILITY     "they've done this for real"
5  ACCOUNTABILITY "someone owns it and will finish"
6  METHOD & EXIT  "defined process, no trap"           ← where the 5 services belong
7  SMALL STEP     "starting costs me almost nothing"
```

**REVISED 2026-08-19 — two additions, no reordering.**

- **Stage 3 is no longer "cost".** It is *"this costs me something I care about"* — which for a
  contented operator is **job quality or time, not job volume.** All four competitors sell growth to
  a buyer segment that does not want it.
- **Four entry points, not one:** search → stage 1 · **post-cold-call → stage 1, mid-conversation** ·
  referred → ~stage 4 · **eager → stage 7**.
- **An express path must exist at every stage.** The eager buyer (F1 item 9) is the most valuable
  visitor and must never complete education to reach contact — **78% of customers buy from the first
  responder** (C6). The fix is a bypass, not a shorter sequence.
- **Stage 2 amended:** the obstacle is not only *"they cannot see it"* but *"they do not want to
  look."* Diagnosis must arrive as **checkable facts, never as a correction of their stated
  position.**

**Not reorderable** — each stage supplies the next stage's precondition. **A belief order, not a
scroll order, and not a gate:** 🚫 it does not mandate eight sections, does not require traversal, and
determines nothing about layout, hierarchy, hero, type, colour, imagery or motion.

Skyler's example order was **not** adopted: credibility is stage 0 not 4; value cannot be asserted at
stage 3; and "proof" is two different proofs doing two jobs at two moments.

## 15. 🔴 What the research CONTRADICTS from previous Kreated assumptions

### 15.1 "Founder-led / no account managers" is not a differentiator
**Evidence: HIGH (E2, E3).** Tyndall: *"You won't be passed to account managers — you'll work with me
on strategy and results."* Peak City: *"You get my cell phone number."* Both back it with 150 clients
/ 13 years and 700 sites / 15 years.

Kreated's most-repeated distinguishing claim is **already owned, more plainly, by better-credentialed
competitors.** It is a bar to clear, not a position to hold.

### 15.2 "We close the gap…" is directionally right and commercially mis-aimed
**Evidence: MEDIUM-HIGH.** The underlying mechanism is real — 50ms credibility, aesthetics raising
judged credibility (A3, A5). But the buyer's *stated* problem is **calls and jobs**, not looks. The
gap framing sells appearance; the buyer buys **the phone ringing**.

⚠️ Consistent with [`../docs/DECISIONS.md`](../docs/DECISIONS.md) 013 keeping it as
`HISTORICAL / COPY CANDIDATE`.

### 15.3 The Kreated Method is demoted from headline to support
**Evidence: MEDIUM (A6).** Process appears in agency-selection criteria but always **behind** people,
transparency and evidence. V1 led with the Method; the research places method at **stage 6 of 7**.

### 15.4 V1's load-bearing vocabulary is not the buyer's vocabulary
**Evidence: MEDIUM (first-party).** V1 designated `system` · `evidence` · `visibility` · `conversion`
· `foundations` · `scoped` · `documented` as load-bearing
([`../docs/BRAND.md`](../docs/BRAND.md) §C). **Kreated's one real satisfied client used none of
them.**

### 15.5 🔴 The homepage Work decision has a measurable persuasion cost
**Evidence: MEDIUM-HIGH (A6).**

[`../docs/DECISIONS.md`](../docs/DECISIONS.md) 007/008 set homepage Work candidates as **LLEC +
Rare Raleigh**. Against trust requirement 3 (capability):
- **LLEC** — real, live, screenshottable, **no case-study narrative** by design;
- **Rare Raleigh** — has the narrative, **no completed implementation**, may never imply one;
- **LearnSmart** — the only full case study, only live site with a testimonial and reported outcome
  — **is off the homepage.**

**Stage 4 is therefore the stage the current plan serves least well.**

⚠️ **A genuinely favourable reading exists and may fully answer this:** if Stage 2 (diagnosis) is the
hinge, then **Rare Raleigh — a documented audit-and-plan-before-build engagement — is literal proof
of the diagnostic capability**, and LLEC proves delivery. **Rare Raleigh proves diagnosis; LLEC
proves delivery.** Together they cover the sequence better than they appear to.

🚫 Not overridden here. Reported for Skyler.

### 15.6 The rating rule needs a second look in market context
**Evidence: HIGH (A1, E1).** DECISION 017 permits `Rated 5 Stars` and accurate star glyphs subject to
evidence and design approval. But **47% of consumers won't consider a business with under 20
reviews**, and **74% want reviews from the last three months** (A1). Kreated has **one** review,
older than that window, while a direct competitor displays *"Rated 5.0 on Google · Zero Negative
Reviews"* (E1).

🔴 **A 5-star display invites the count comparison Kreated loses.** Finding, not a decision.

### 15.7 Conversion priority may be inverted
**Evidence: MEDIUM.** V1's order — `Start Your Project` primary, audit secondary — asks a buyer who
**cannot self-diagnose** (B8, C1) to make a decision they are not equipped to make, while the real
competitor is inertia. ⚠️ Also: `Start Your Project` is **not distinctive** — NC Digital uses "Start
Your Website Project" (E4).

### 15.8 "Honest positioning" needs to operate as verifiability, not disclosure
**Evidence: MEDIUM.** V1's strategy was to be conspicuously honest. The research supports the
instinct — the market is saturated with unverifiable confidence (C10) — but the mechanism matters:
buyers reward **claims they can check**, not **admissions of what is missing**. ⚠️ At 50ms, evidence
labels can read as weakness rather than rigour. **Whether honesty reads as rigour or as apology is
decided by Stage 0, not by the labels.**

### 15.9 The "no team" fear may be over-corrected
**Evidence: LOW-MEDIUM.** V1 permanently rejected keywords like `Raleigh SEO company` because they
imply a team ([`../docs/SEO.md`](../docs/SEO.md) §2). Solo/founder access is now openly *sold* by
competitors (E2, E3), suggesting the market reads solo as a feature. ⚠️ Flagged only — the ban costs
little and the honesty rule stands.

## The one-paragraph version

> Kreated's buyer is an owner-operated local service business where one job is worth thousands, who
> is losing work during a customer research process they never see. Cold-called, they say they are
> consistent, content, mostly word-of-mouth, and already have a website — and they are often right.
> They never mention price, because the conversation ends first. Approached at the moment something
> has actually gone wrong, they become a different person: searching, comparing, and sceptical of a
> market that is loud, identical and pre-discounted, where everyone claims results, offers a free
> audit, and promises founder access. Kreated cannot out-claim any of it with three clients and one
> review. What it can do is be the only one that hands them a fact they can check for themselves,
> before selling anything, on a site whose competence is legible in fifty milliseconds — and then get
> out of the way of the ones who are already ready. **Checkable truth is the opening. Execution is the
> proof. Everything else is table stakes.**
