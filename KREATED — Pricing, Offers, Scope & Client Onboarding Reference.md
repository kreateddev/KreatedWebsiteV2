# 2. Core Pricing Philosophy

Kreated is **not** positioned as a budget website company.

Kreated should occupy the space between:

1. freelancers / commodity website providers
2. established boutique marketing agencies

The current positioning is:

> **Boutique-agency strategy and implementation at a meaningful discount to established agency pricing.**

The purpose of the discount is to reflect Kreated's current stage while it accumulates:

- outside-client case studies
- verified reviews
- traffic results
- ranking improvements
- qualified lead data
- conversion improvements
- booked-estimate data
- attributed revenue
- repeatable vertical expertise

The goal is **not to remain permanently cheaper than the market**.

Pricing should rise as proof rises.

# 3. Market Research Basis

Pricing decisions should be benchmarked against **real agencies with identifiable customer proof**, not against websites that simply claim to be agencies.

Valid benchmarking evidence includes:

- verified client reviews
- named client portfolios
- detailed case studies
- independently identifiable customers
- disclosed customer spend
- measurable campaign results
- established operating history
- credible third-party profiles

Do not use an agency as an important market benchmark solely because:

- its website looks professional
- it claims a large portfolio
- it publishes package prices
- it ranks for marketing-related search terms
- it appears to have been generated quickly with AI
- it cannot demonstrate actual client work

# 8. Website Offer Philosophy

A website project and an SEO retainer are separate products.

A website project establishes the infrastructure needed to:

- present the company professionally
- explain services
- establish trust
- convert visitors
- measure leads
- support search visibility
- establish proper site architecture
- create the base from which future marketing can grow

An ongoing SEO program continuously expands and improves that infrastructure.

Do not promise months of ongoing SEO work inside a one-time website package unless specifically included in the proposal.
