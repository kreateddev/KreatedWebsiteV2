# Retired: the KWLG header monogram

Removed from 14 inner-page headers on 2026-08-30 during the whole-site visual
refinement pass, so that one wordmark treatment runs across all 17 routes.

## Why it went

The homepage, /free-website-audit/ and /thanks/ carried a plain Cormorant
wordmark. The other 14 routes carried this monogram PLUS a wordmark at a
different weight, size and tracking — so the brand visibly reset on every
navigation away from the homepage.

`docs/BRAND.md` was the tiebreaker. It records the KREATED wordmark as *the*
identity (line 20) and lists the monogram as an OPEN QUESTION, not an approved
lock: "Does the KWLG monogram become part of the V2 identity, and does
Cormorant + monogram lock together?" (line 157). With no approved lock, the
brief's default applied: the homepage treatment is the source of truth, and a
mark the homepage does not use is not forced onto inner pages.

## This is an owner decision, not a closed one

If the monogram IS adopted, it belongs on all 17 routes including the homepage,
at one size and one relationship to the wordmark. Do not reinstate it on a
subset — that is the exact condition this removal fixed.
