# Homepage Services + Process stages — removed 2026-08-30

Removed from the homepage in the Homepage Refinement pass, **not deleted**.

## Why

The homepage was trying to sell, explain, demonstrate and prove at once. Under the
locked hierarchy — homepage sells, `/work/` proves, service pages explain — the two
large interactive stages were the homepage doing the other pages' jobs, and they were
competing with the real client work for attention. Together they were 90kB of the
homepage's 145kB.

## What is here

- `homepage-services-stage.html` — the five Ashford & Sons scenes (`.svc__stage`).
- `homepage-process-viz.html` — the five process artefacts (`.proc__viz`), including
  the Discover audit sheet, the GBP/site pair and the vibrating phone.

Both are approved 2026-08-27 art direction and are inline SVG using page fonts. The
CSS that drives them is still in `styles.css` — nothing was deleted from there — so
either can be reinstated by pasting the markup back into its section.

## Ashford & Sons

Both files contain the labelled ILLUSTRATIVE non-client specimen (`docs/PROOF.md` §8).
It is a **concept demonstration, never delivered client work.** If either stage is ever
reused anywhere, its demonstration label travels with it.
