What it checks on a contractor’s site

The six areas the result is organized by.

  • Website

    Whether the homepage says what you do and gives a homeowner an obvious next step: a number that dials, a form, a button that asks for the job.

  • Search foundations

    Titles, headings and the description search results show, and whether each service you sell has a page of its own.

  • Local visibility

    Whether the towns you work in are named, whether the ones you name have pages, and whether the business is marked up for search engines with a phone that dials.

  • Brand and trust

    Whether the homepage shows what a stranger looks for before letting you in the house: reviews, licensing, insurance, warranties, years in business.

  • Answer readiness

    Whether the site answers questions plainly enough for a search engine or an AI assistant to describe you correctly.

  • Measurement

    Whether anything is installed that would tell you where your calls and forms actually come from.

It reads your public website, and only that. It cannot open your Google Business Profile, your reviews on Google, your analytics or your Search Console, so anything on the local side is judged from what your pages say publicly. The result names what it did not inspect.

Questions

Does it work for any kind of contractor?

It reads any public website. The checks are the ones that decide whether a trades site gets the call: what you do, where you work, how easy you are to reach, and whether there is anything on the page to trust. They apply the same way to roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, general contracting or any other trade, and to businesses outside the trades as well.

Can it see my Google Business Profile or my Google reviews?

No. It reads your public website and nothing else. It cannot open your Google Business Profile, your reviews on Google, your analytics or your Search Console, and the result says what it did not inspect rather than guessing.

Is it actually free, or is it a sales call in disguise?

Free, and there is no call attached to it. The result appears on the page, and if nothing needs doing, that is what it says.

Can I send the result to someone?

Yes. Every finished audit has its own link, kept for 180 days, so you can send it to a partner or an office manager. It holds the findings only, nothing you typed into the form.
