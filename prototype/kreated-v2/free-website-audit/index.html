What the audit checks

Six areas, the same six a build has to get right.

  • Clarity

    Whether the site says what you do, who it is for and where you work, before anyone has to scroll or guess.

  • The path to contacting you

    How many steps it takes to reach you, how obvious it is, and whether it survives on a phone.

  • Mobile

    How the site behaves at real phone sizes, which for local service work is usually the only screen it is judged on.

  • Trust

    The pieces a customer looks for before calling a stranger, and the obvious ones that are missing.

  • Local discoverability

    Whether your pages, your market and your public business information agree about what you do and where.

  • Technical friction

    Titles, headings, page structure and the plumbing that quietly decides whether a page can be found at all.

What you get

  • Findings

    Six areas, each with what was found and the evidence it was found from. Not a score.

  • Priority

    What is costing you inquiries now, what is worth doing next, and what is already fine and needs no money.

Questions

Is it actually free, or is it a sales call in disguise?

Free, and there is no call attached to it. You submit the site, the audit runs, and the result appears on the page. If nothing needs doing, that is what it says.

Do I have to buy anything afterward?

No. If you want to go further, the routes are Start a Project or one of the six services. If you do not, nothing happens.

What do I need to send?

Your website address and a sentence about what is bothering you. The rest of the form exists so the result can reach you, and the more you say about what you want the site to do, the more useful the answer.

How long does it take?

Usually a few minutes. It runs after you submit and the result appears on this page in the same visit. You are not waiting on an email or on someone getting back to you.

What if my site is fine, or I am not a good fit for Kreated?

You still get the complete audit. It is not withheld or trimmed because a submission is unlikely to become a project, and if Kreated is probably not the right partner to do the work, the audit says so rather than steering you.

Is this the same as an SEO audit?

No. It looks at search fundamentals as one of six areas, not as a keyword study or a technical crawl. If search is the whole problem, that conversation is Local SEO.
