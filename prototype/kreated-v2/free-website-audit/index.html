Free Website Audit

A look at your site and your Google presence.

Send the site you already have. The audit runs after you submit and the result appears here in the same visit: what is working, what is not, and what would actually be worth doing first.

  • Your audit is generated after you submit, usually a few minutes
  • The result appears on this page, in the same visit
  • You get the whole thing, even if Kreated is not the right fit to do the work

Who it’s for

  • You have a site and you are not sure it is doing its job It exists, people occasionally mention it, and nothing about it tells you whether it is helping or quietly costing you work.
  • You are deciding between patching and rebuilding Worth knowing which one the site actually needs before spending money on either.
  • You are being found less than you think you should be Or found for the wrong things, or not in the places customers look first.

If you do not have a website yet, this is the wrong starting point. There is nothing to review. Web Design & Development is where that conversation starts.

What gets looked at

The same things a build would have to get right.

  • Clarity: whether the site says what you do, for whom, and where, before anyone has to scroll or guess.
  • The path to contacting you: how many steps it takes, how obvious it is, and whether it survives on a phone.
  • Mobile: how it behaves at real phone sizes, which for local service work is usually the first and only screen it is judged on.
  • Search fundamentals, titles, headings, page structure and whether each page has a job, rather than a full keyword study.
  • Local presence, whether the site and your public business information agree about what you do and where you work.
  • Trust gaps, the obvious missing pieces a customer looks for before calling a stranger.

What happens after you send it

  1. You submit the form The site address and one line about what is bothering you. The rest is optional and only makes the result more specific.
  2. The audit runs It usually takes a few minutes. You stay on this page while it works. There is nothing to check later and nothing to wait for in an inbox.
  3. The result appears here What stood out, what is worth doing first, and what is not worth bothering with. If your site is in good shape, that is what it says.

You get the complete audit either way. If Kreated is probably not the right partner to carry out the work, the audit says that too. And still tells you what to do.

No cost, and no obligation on either side. Sending it does not put you in a sales sequence.

Questions

Is it actually free, or is it a sales call in disguise?

Free, and there is no call attached to it. You submit the site, the audit runs, and the result appears on the page. If nothing needs doing, that is what it says.

Do I have to buy anything afterwards?

No. If you want to go further, the routes are Start a Project or one of the six services. If you don’t, nothing happens.

What do I need to send?

Your website address and a sentence about what is bothering you. The rest of the form is optional and exists so the reply can be more specific, the more you say about what you want the site to do, the more useful the answer.

How long does it take?

Usually a few minutes. It runs after you submit and the result appears on this page in the same visit. You are not waiting on an email or on someone getting back to you.

What if my site is fine, or I’m not a good fit for Kreated?

You still get the complete audit. It is not withheld or trimmed because a submission is unlikely to become a project, and if Kreated is probably not the right partner to do the work, the audit says so rather than steering you.

Is this the same as an SEO audit?

No. It looks at search fundamentals as one of six areas, not as a keyword study or a technical crawl. If search is the whole problem, that conversation is Local SEO.
