Both images are real captures of the same business’s website, taken before and after. The site is live and open to inspection.

The situation

Restoration is an urgent, local, high-trust purchase. The buyer searches on a phone, compares a small number of options quickly, and calls whoever looks most capable of handling the situation in front of them.

The capability was never the question. The website was: it had to make the case on a small screen, in the few seconds before the next contractor gets the call.

Buyer behaviour documented from this engagement, for restoration and urgent home services. It is not established for other verticals.

What Kreated delivered

One person did the design and the build.

  • Structure and navigation
  • Responsive layout, phone first
  • The contact path
