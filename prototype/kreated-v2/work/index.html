What these show

And what they deliberately do not.

Each project here is a real business with a live site you can open and check. What is listed against each one is the scope that was actually delivered, not everything that could have been.

Where something is measurable it is labeled, dated and attributed. Where it was reported by the client rather than verified, it says so. Where no evidence exists, nothing is claimed. That is why these read as records rather than results pages.

What they are useful for is judging the work itself: how a site is structured, how clearly a business explains itself, and what was delivered against what was scoped.
