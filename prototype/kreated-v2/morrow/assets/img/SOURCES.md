## Brand elements in photographs

Two images carry the Morrow mark: the door glass in the exterior and the
takeaway cup. Both are composited by `tools/build-images.py` (`overlays` in
the manifest) onto stock photographs. They are concept mockups and are
described as such in the case study; they must not be presented as
photographs of real Morrow collateral.

## Treatment

One restrained grade is applied in the pipeline so 27 images from 20
photographers read as one shoot: saturation −8%, blacks lifted to about
`#231B16`, whites held under clip, highlights nudged warm, shadows nudged
olive. Film grain stays in CSS. Originals are never modified.

## Rejected after review

Recorded so they are not re-sourced: latte-art hearts and glass cups
(Pexels 30556589–30556593), branded storefronts (Leu Leu 32006076, Casual
Coffee 29156074), the Gene Café roaster (33061145, visible brand), coffee
beans as decoration (11552880, 9535503), top-down flat lays throughout,
and every frame with a person facing the camera.
