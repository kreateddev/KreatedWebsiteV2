What it is

Three words that get used interchangeably and should not be.

Brand strategy is the decision layer: who the business is for, what it stands for, what position it is trying to own and how it competes. Brand identity is how those decisions are expressed, visually and in words. Marketing is how the resulting offer reaches people.

They run in that order for a reason. An identity built without the strategy underneath it produces something that looks considered and says nothing in particular. Most businesses that describe a marketing problem are describing a strategy problem instead: the work is good, but nothing has decided what the business is claiming or who it is claiming it to.

Is this just a logo? No. A logo is one element inside an identity, which also covers colour, typography, how photography is treated, how the business writes and speaks, and the rules that keep every surface looking like the same company.

When to rebrand rather than refresh. A refresh is right when the business is still the same business and the presentation has aged. A new identity is right when what the business does, who it serves or what it claims has genuinely changed, and the current identity now describes a company that no longer exists.

What you actually get

Not a mood board. A set of decisions you can hand to anyone.

Branding gets vague when it is described as a feeling. In practice it is a short list of decisions, what the mark is, how the name is set, which two typefaces do which jobs, which colours are allowed where, written down clearly enough that the next person who touches your website, your van or your estimate sheet does not have to guess.

Most of the cost of weak branding is not aesthetic. It is that every new thing has to be reinvented, and each version drifts a little further from the last.

What this is not

Worth saying out loud.

  • Not a slogan service. A tagline is written when there is something true to say, not to fill a line under the mark.
  • Not a rebrand for its own sake. If the existing mark is working, a refresh is cheaper and usually righter than a replacement.
  • Not a promise about how customers will feel. No recognition, recall or preference figure is claimed here, because none is measured.
  • Not print production management. Files are supplied; printers, signage and vehicle wraps are arranged by you.
  • Not the website. Branding is an add-on to a website project, bundled with one, or a separate engagement.

Questions

Do I need branding, or just a logo?

If you need one file for one purpose, you need a logo. If the same business has to look like itself on a website, an estimate, a van and a sign, you need the decisions around the logo too, the type, the colour, and the rules for using them.

A Brand Refresh from $750 is the smaller version of that.

Can I get branding and a website together?

Yes, and it is usually the cheaper order of operations. Brand + Website typically runs $3,950–$5,950+ depending on brand scope, website package and copy needs. Doing the identity first means the site is built from settled decisions rather than provisional ones.

Will you write my tagline?

Only if there is something true to say. A messaging foundation is part of Brand Identity, and that covers how the business describes itself. A slogan invented to fill space under a logo is not an asset.

We already have a logo. Is it wasted?

Usually not. A Brand Refresh exists for exactly this: cleaning up or refining what you have, settling the typography and colour around it, and writing the short guide that stops it drifting again.

After final payment you own the finished work and the design and content created specifically for you. Ask, and you get the project files that go with it, the vector logo files, the working files, and the exports you need to hand to a printer or a sign maker.

What is the difference between brand strategy, brand identity and marketing?

Brand strategy decides who the business is for, what it stands for and what position it is trying to own. Brand identity expresses those decisions visually and in words. Marketing puts the resulting offer in front of people.

They work in that order. An identity built without the strategy underneath looks considered and says nothing in particular.

When should a business rebrand rather than refresh?

Refresh when the business is still the same business and the presentation has aged. Rebuild the identity when what the business does, who it serves or what it claims has genuinely changed, and the current identity now describes a company that no longer exists.
