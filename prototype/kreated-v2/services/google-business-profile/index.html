What it is

Before any of the detail.

The Google Business Profile is the panel that appears beside or above the search results when somebody looks up your business or a service you provide nearby. This is the work of creating it, claiming it, getting it verified and making it accurate.

For most local buyers the profile is what they see before the website, and often instead of it. Hours, service area, categories, photos and reviews are read there and a decision is frequently made there. Getting it accurate and complete is usually the highest-return hour anyone spends on local search.

What the work covers

The parts, named.

Creation, claiming and verification. Setting the profile up where there is not one, claiming it where it exists but is not yours to edit, and guidance through Google’s verification process. Verification is Google’s procedure and its timing is theirs, not Kreated’s.

Optimization. A one-time audit and correction of the profile: categories, services, description, service area, hours, photos and the details that are quietly wrong. Inherited and half-finished profiles are the normal case rather than the exception.

Ongoing management, where it belongs. Categories and services kept refined, and posts where they are genuinely worth making. This is not sold as a standalone subscription — ongoing profile work sits inside local search engagements, alongside everything else that has to move together.

Who it’s for

And what it will not do.

A business with a profile that was set up quickly, inherited from somebody who has left, or never finished — and that wants it correct without committing to a monthly programme. The one-time correction is deliberately one time. Buying it does not commit you to anything ongoing.

It is not a ranking guarantee. A complete, accurate profile is a prerequisite for local visibility, not a purchase of it. What position you hold depends on competition, proximity and a great deal Google does not publish.

It is not review generation. Reviews are earned from customers. Nothing here manufactures them, and any service that offers to should be treated as a liability rather than a supplier.

It is not a substitute for a website. A profile pointing at a site that does not explain the business is a limited asset. The two are worked on together for that reason.

Questions

I don’t have a profile yet. Can you set one up?

If you do not have a profile yet, we help you create, claim, and verify it first. Then we complete the profile using the steps below.

Can you guarantee Google will verify my business?

No. Verification is Google’s decision and no agency controls it. What is offered is guidance through the process, getting the information right, knowing what Google asks for, and knowing what to do when a verification attempt fails.

Is this ongoing management?

No, and this is worth being exact about. A Growth project includes a one-time audit and optimization of an existing profile. It is not monthly management, and monthly management is not included in Growth. Continuing profile work, posts, ongoing updates, review monitoring. Belongs inside the broader recurring local engagement rather than being bought as a profile retainer on its own.

Will this get me into the top three results?

No one can promise that, and nothing on this site does. A complete, accurate, consistent profile is the part that is within anyone’s control. Where a profile appears is not. If the profile is not appearing at all, why your business may not be showing on Google Maps covers the causes, including the one nobody can fix.

Do you write or buy reviews?

No. Reviews are written by customers or they are worthless. What this service builds is the process, a repeatable way to ask at the point a customer is most likely to say yes.

Can I buy profile management on its own, monthly?

Not as a standalone product. There is no separate monthly Google Business Profile package, and rather than invent a price for one, the honest answer is that ongoing profile work sits inside the wider local program, Local Growth at $900/month, on a three-month initial commitment and month-to-month after that. The one-time audit and optimization is built into a Growth website project at $2,950. Every engagement is scoped and priced in writing before it begins.

What can you change, and what does Google control?

Workable: the primary category, which is the strongest signal a profile owner controls, plus secondary categories, services, descriptions, photos, hours, the consistency of the business information, and a repeatable way of asking for and answering reviews. Google's: which verification method is offered and whether it succeeds, how far the searcher is from the business, and the ranking itself.

Why can nobody guarantee the map results?

Because local results are influenced by relevance, distance and prominence, and distance cannot be changed by anyone. A profile can be made more relevant and more complete. It cannot be made closer to the person searching.
