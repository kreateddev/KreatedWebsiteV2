What it is

And where the boundary of control sits.

Google Business Profile optimization is the work of creating, claiming and completing the business listing that appears in Google Maps and local search results, so that it describes the business accurately and completely.

The important thing to understand before buying this from anyone is that it is split work. Some of it is genuinely in the hands of whoever manages the profile. The rest belongs to Google.

What can be worked on: the primary category, which is the single strongest signal a profile owner controls, and secondary categories for services genuinely offered; the services and descriptions; photographs; hours, including the ones people check on holidays; the business information itself and whether it matches the website and other listings; a repeatable way of asking for and responding to reviews; and how the profile and the website support each other.

What Google controls: which verification method is offered and whether verification succeeds; how far the searcher is from the business, which nobody can change; and the ranking itself. Local results are influenced by relevance, distance and prominence, and distance is simply not something any agency can affect.

So this service improves the things that can be improved and reports the rest honestly. It does not include a promise of verification, and it does not include a promise of a position in the local results.

Who it’s for

The profile is often met before the website is.

  • Businesses with no profile yet Creation, claiming and guidance through Google’s verification come first, and then the profile gets completed properly rather than left half-filled.
  • Businesses with a profile nobody has touched in years Wrong category, half the services missing, a description written once and never revisited, a service area that no longer matches where the work actually happens.
  • Businesses whose profile and website disagree Different services listed in each, a different area, a different phone number. Two sources telling a customer two things is worse than one telling them nothing.

The same disagreement matters beyond a customer reading two pages. Systems that summarize a business now assemble an answer from several sources at once, and they have no way to decide which version is right. Making those sources agree is the starting point of Answer Engine Optimization.

How the work runs

A pass through the profile, field by field, checked against the site.

  1. Create, claim, or get access If there is no profile, it is created and claimed, with guidance through Google’s verification. Google decides verification, not Kreated.
  2. Business information Name, contact details, service area and hours reviewed against what the business actually does today.
  3. Categories and services The primary category, the secondary ones, and the service list: the fields that most often decide which searches a profile is eligible for at all.
  4. Description and photos A description that reads like the business, and guidance on the photography a profile actually benefits from.
  5. Reviews, as a process A repeatable way to ask, at the moment a customer is most likely to say yes. Reviews are earned, not supplied.
  6. Checked against the website The last pass is consistency: the profile and the site saying the same thing about services, area and contact details.

Questions

I don’t have a profile yet. Can you set one up?

If you do not have a profile yet, we help you create, claim, and verify it first. Then we complete the profile using the steps below.

Can you guarantee Google will verify my business?

No. Verification is Google’s decision and no agency controls it. What is offered is guidance through the process, getting the information right, knowing what Google asks for, and knowing what to do when a verification attempt fails.

Is this ongoing management?

No, and this is worth being exact about. A Growth project includes a one-time audit and optimization of an existing profile. It is not monthly management, and monthly management is not included in Growth.

Continuing profile work, posts, ongoing updates, review monitoring. Belongs inside the broader recurring local engagement rather than being bought as a profile retainer on its own.

Will this get me into the top three results?

No one can promise that, and nothing on this site does. A complete, accurate, consistent profile is the part that is within anyone’s control. Where a profile appears is not.

If the profile is not appearing at all, why your business may not be showing on Google Maps covers the causes, including the one nobody can fix.

Do you write or buy reviews?

No. Reviews are written by customers or they are worthless. What this service builds is the process, a repeatable way to ask at the point a customer is most likely to say yes.

Can I buy profile management on its own, monthly?

Not as a standalone product. There is no separate monthly Google Business Profile package, and rather than invent a price for one, the honest answer is that ongoing profile work sits inside the wider local program, Local Growth at $900/month, on a three-month initial commitment and month-to-month after that.

The one-time audit and optimization is built into a Growth website project at $2,950. Every engagement is scoped and priced in writing before it begins.

What can you change, and what does Google control?

Workable: the primary category, which is the strongest signal a profile owner controls, plus secondary categories, services, descriptions, photos, hours, the consistency of the business information, and a repeatable way of asking for and answering reviews.

Google's: which verification method is offered and whether it succeeds, how far the searcher is from the business, and the ranking itself.

Why can nobody guarantee the map results?

Because local results are influenced by relevance, distance and prominence, and distance cannot be changed by anyone. A profile can be made more relevant and more complete. It cannot be made closer to the person searching.
