What it is

The panel buyers see before your website

The Google Business Profile is the panel that appears beside or above the search results when somebody looks up your business or a service you provide nearby. This is the work of creating it, claiming it, getting it verified and making it accurate.

For most local buyers the profile is what they see before the website, and often instead of it. Hours, service area, categories, photos and reviews are read there and a decision is frequently made there. Getting it accurate and complete is usually the highest-return hour anyone spends on local search.

Questions

The profile, answered

I don’t have a profile yet. Can you set one up?

If you do not have a profile yet, Kreated helps you create, claim and verify it first, then completes the profile using the steps below.

Can you guarantee Google will verify my business?

No. Verification is Google’s decision and no agency controls it. What is offered is guidance through the process, getting the information right, knowing what Google asks for, and knowing what to do when a verification attempt fails.

Is this ongoing management?

No, and this is worth being exact about. A Growth project includes a one-time audit and optimization of an existing profile. It is not monthly management, and monthly management is not included in Growth. Continuing profile work (posts, ongoing updates, review monitoring) is part of Local Presence at $500/month or Local Growth at $900/month.

Will this get me into the top three results?

No one can promise that, and nothing on this site does. A complete, accurate, consistent profile is the part that is within anyone’s control. Where a profile appears is not. If the profile is not appearing at all, why your business may not be showing on Google Maps covers the causes, including the one nobody can fix.

Do you write or buy reviews?

No. Reviews are written by customers or they are worthless. What this service builds is the process, a repeatable way to ask at the point a customer is most likely to say yes.

Can I buy profile management on its own, monthly?

Yes. Local Presence, at $500/month, is the monthly option for keeping the profile accurate: profile management, reviews, citations and monitoring. Local Growth, at $900/month on a three-month initial commitment and month-to-month after that, includes it alongside the wider search work. The one-time fix is Profile Optimization from $450, and it is also built into a Growth website project from $2,950. Every engagement is scoped and priced in writing before it begins.

What can you change, and what does Google control?

Workable: the primary category, which is the strongest signal a profile owner controls, plus secondary categories, services, descriptions, photos, hours, the consistency of the business information, and a repeatable way of asking for and answering reviews. Google's: which verification method is offered and whether it succeeds, how far the searcher is from the business, and the ranking itself.

Why can nobody guarantee the map results?

Because local results are influenced by relevance, distance and prominence, and distance cannot be changed by anyone. A profile can be made more relevant and more complete. It cannot be made closer to the person searching.
