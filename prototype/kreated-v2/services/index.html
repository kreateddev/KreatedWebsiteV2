Which one you need

Four pairs that get confused, settled in a sentence each.

Web Design or Website Redesign. A redesign keeps what already works: the pages earning attention, the content that is already correct, the addresses those pages live at. Choose web design when there is nothing worth keeping, or nothing there at all.

Local SEO or Google Business Profile. The profile is the business listing itself, in maps and local results. Local SEO is the wider system around it, most of which lives on the website. The profile is one surface inside that system, and it is priced on its own because it can be worth doing alone.

Local SEO or Answer Engine Optimization. Local search competes for a position when someone nearby is looking. Answer engine work is about being understood and described correctly when a search engine or an AI assistant summarizes the business. They share the same prerequisites, and local search usually comes first.

Brand Strategy or marketing. Brand strategy decides who the business is for and what it claims. Marketing puts that claim in front of people. Businesses that describe a marketing problem often have a strategy problem, because nothing has decided what is being said or to whom.
