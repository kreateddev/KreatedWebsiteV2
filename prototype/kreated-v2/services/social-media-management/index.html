What it is

Before any of the detail.

Social media management is the work of keeping a business’s Facebook, Instagram and short-video accounts active with posts worth looking at: edited, captioned, scheduled and published every month.

Plenty of customers look a business up on Facebook or Instagram before they call, to see recent work and whether the business is still active. An account that last posted two years ago answers that question badly, however good the work is.

This is the organic side of social media marketing: the business’s own accounts, kept active every month. Paid social advertising is not part of it.

What the work covers

Two ways to work, one monthly output.

Social Management, $900/month. You and your crew take the photos and clips on the job and send them over. Kreated does everything after that: the editing, the captions, the scheduling and the posting.

Social Production, $1,800/month. Everything in Social Management, plus the content planned and made by Kreated: the ideas, the photography and the video.

The monthly output. Twelve posts and four short videos a month in both options, across Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok or YouTube Shorts.

Editing and captions. Photos and footage edited into posts and short videos, with a caption written for every one.

Scheduling and posting. Posts go out on a schedule rather than whenever somebody remembers, so the accounts do not go quiet.

Who it’s for

And what it will not do.

Social Management suits a business that already takes photos and video on the job and has nobody with time to turn them into posts. Social Production suits a business where nobody will film consistently, or where the work deserves better pictures than a phone in a hurry.

It is not a follower guarantee. What a platform shows, and who follows, likes or calls, is not in anyone’s control. Posting consistently, with content worth looking at, is.

It is not paid advertising. Both options are organic posting on the business’s own accounts. Nothing here buys reach.

It is not a substitute for a website. A post sends someone looking; the website and the Google Business Profile are usually where they decide.

Questions

Do I have to film anything?

That depends on which option you choose. With Social Management, you and your crew take the photos and clips on the job and send them over, and Kreated edits, captions, schedules and posts them. With Social Production, Kreated also plans the content and does the photography and video.

Which platforms does it cover?

Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok or YouTube Shorts for short video. The same platforms in both options.

How much gets posted?

Twelve posts and four short videos a month, in both options. The difference between them is who makes the photos and footage, not how much is posted.

What does it cost?

Social Management is $900/month: you send the photos and footage, and Kreated edits, captions and posts them. Social Production is $1,800/month and adds content ideas, photography and video by Kreated. Both run on a three-month initial commitment and month-to-month after that. Every engagement is scoped and priced in writing before it begins.

Can you guarantee followers, likes or leads?

No. What a platform shows and who follows, likes or calls is not in anyone’s control, and nothing on this site promises it. What is in Kreated’s control is posting consistently, on schedule, with content worth looking at.

Does this include paid ads?

No. Both options are organic posting on the business’s own accounts. Paid social advertising is not part of either.
