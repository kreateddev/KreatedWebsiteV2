Which website package is you

  1. You have no website. People search your name, find a profile on someone else's platform, and decide from that. Start at One Page Website.
  2. You have a website and it embarrasses you. It exists, it is out of date, and you would rather not send anyone to it. That is Launch.
  3. Your website is fine and the phone still does not ring. The work is good, the site describes it, and competitors are the ones being found. That is Growth, because the answer is research before it is design.
  4. You sell several things, or sell in several places, and already spend to win work. Leads need routing, qualifying and tracing back to source. That is Market Leader.

If two of these describe you, the cheaper one is usually right. Every engagement is built on the same foundation, so moving up later is an expansion rather than a rebuild, and nothing is paid for twice.

What moves the number

  • How many pages actually earn their place. Not a page count you pick: the number of distinct things you sell, and the number of places you sell them, where the search demand is real.
  • Who writes it. Copy support is included everywhere. Full copy written from interviews, for every page, is more work than tidying what you have.
  • What already exists. A site with usable structure and content behind it is faster than one that has to be reconstructed. An older site with links worth keeping needs a redirect map, and that is real work either way.
  • Photography. Real pictures of your work change a site more than any other single input. Coordinating a shoot is scoped separately; using what you already have costs nothing.
  • What it has to connect to. A form that emails you is not the same job as a form that creates a record in your CRM, routes by service, and books time.
  • How competitive the area is. Being found in a market where nobody has done this work is a different amount of work from being found where everybody has.

None of this is estimated on a call. It is scoped in writing, with the number on it, before anything starts.
