KREATED / PRIVACY

Privacy

What this site collects, what it does not, and where anything you send actually goes. It describes how the website behaves. It is not legal advice.

What the site loads

Nothing from anyone else. Every font, image and script on this site is served from this domain. There is no analytics tag, no content-delivery network, no embedded map, no video player, no chat widget and no social button. Opening a page here contacts no other company.

The site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.

What the forms collect

Two forms exist, and each collects only what it shows you:

Start a project
Name, business, email, phone, website, what you need, an approximate range, and anything you write in the message box.
Free website audit
Name, business, email, phone, website, and the problem you describe.

Only the name and email are required. Everything else is optional and the form works without it.

Where a submission goes

Form submissions are handled by Netlify, which hosts this site, and are read by Skyler Reyes. Replies come from contact@kreated.dev. Nothing you send is sold, and nothing is passed to an advertiser or a data broker.

What is measured

Today, nothing. The site contains the wiring for analytics but no analytics service is connected, so no measurement is recorded.

If one is connected later, the events it records carry the name of the form and two yes/no flags, whether a website and a phone number were filled in. They never carry your name, your email, your phone number, your website address or anything you wrote.

Asking about your information

Email contact@kreated.dev and ask what has been kept, or ask for it to be deleted.

Still being settled

Two things are not decided yet, and this page will say so until they are rather than guess:

  • How long inquiry emails are kept.
  • Whether an analytics service will be connected at launch, and which.
